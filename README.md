Hypnotic Beds Website
By ST10537456
Project Overview
This project involves the development of a comprehensive website for Hypnotic Beds, a small factory shop based in Jacobs, Durban, South Africa. The website provides a professional digital presence for the business and allows customers to learn more about its products, services, custom work, and contact options.
Hypnotic Beds was established in 2016 and operates as a direct-from-factory business. The website was developed to help the business reach customers beyond its physical location and provide customers with convenient access to product information, prices, services, and enquiry options.
The website development project is structured into multiple phases, with Part 1 focusing on Building the Foundation and Part 2 focusing on Designing the Visuals and Responsive Development.
Information about the business, its products, services, contact details, and branding was obtained from the business owner.
The website reflects the existing Hypnotic Beds brand identity using royal blue, dark blue, gold, light gold, cream, and white. The design focuses on a professional appearance, clear navigation, readable content, and easy access to customer enquiries.
Website Goals and Objectives
Primary Goals
The website has been designed to achieve several key business objectives:
•	Online Presence: Establish a professional online presence for Hypnotic Beds.
•	Product Accessibility: Allow customers to view available beds, furniture products and prices online.
•	Business Visibility: Help potential customers discover Hypnotic Beds outside of its physical factory location.
•	Customer Enquiries: Provide customers with convenient ways to contact the business through telephone, WhatsApp and email.
•	Quotation Requests: Provide a dedicated Quote & Order page for customers who require products or custom work.
•	Service Promotion: Clearly communicate the services offered by Hypnotic Beds.
•	Portfolio Presentation: Display previous products and custom work through a dedicated gallery.
•	Mobile Accessibility: Ensure the website can be used on desktop, tablet and mobile devices.
•	Professional Branding: Maintain a consistent visual identity based on the supplied Hypnotic Beds business branding.
Key Performance Indicators
The success of the website can be evaluated through:
•	Increased customer enquiries
•	Increased WhatsApp and telephone enquiries
•	Customer use of the Quote & Order page
•	Increased awareness of Hypnotic Beds products and services
•	Customer access to product and pricing information
•	Website accessibility across desktop, tablet and mobile devices
•	Improved presentation of the business online
Target Audience
The website specifically caters to:
•	Parents looking for beds and furniture for their families
•	Individuals looking for beds and furniture
•	Families looking for affordable furniture
•	Customers interested in customised furniture
•	Customers within Durban and surrounding areas
•	Customers looking for direct-from-factory products
•	Shop and business owners requiring furniture or custom work
Key Features and Functionality
Essential Pages Structure
The website consists of seven main pages, each serving a specific purpose:
Homepage (index.html): Acts as the main introduction to Hypnotic Beds. It presents the business, branding, key products, and prominent customer enquiry options.
Products & Prices(products.html): Provides information about available beds and furniture products together with pricing information.
Services(services.html): Provides information about the services offered by Hypnotic Beds, including custom work.
Gallery(gallery.html): Displays photographs of beds, furniture, and previous custom work to give customers examples of the products and work provided by the business.
About(about.html): Provides background information about Hypnotic Beds and its business identity.
Contact(contact.html): Provides the business address, telephone numbers, email address, and other contact information.
Quote & Order(quote.html): Provides customers with a dedicated area to request information, enquire about products, and submit custom-work requirements.
Core Functionality
Responsive Design: The website adapts to desktop, tablet, and mobile screen sizes.
Product Information: Customers can view product information and prices.
Gallery Presentation: Product and custom-work images are displayed using a structured gallery layout.
WhatsApp Communication: WhatsApp enquiry/order buttons provide a direct communication option.
Telephone Communication: Call buttons allow customers to contact the business directly from supported devices.
Custom Work Image Upload Preview: Customers can preview an uploaded image when providing information about custom work.
Contact Information: Business contact details are presented throughout relevant sections of the website.
Responsive Navigation: Navigation changes to a mobile-friendly layout on smaller screens.
Visual Branding: The website uses the colours and visual identity supplied for Hypnotic Beds.
Technical Implementation
The website was developed using Visual Studio Code, HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, and GitHub.HTML5 was used to create the semantic structure of the website. CSS3 was used for visual styling, layout, typography, responsive design, and interactive states. The website uses CSS Grid and Flexbox to create structured layouts and media queries to adapt the design to different screen sizes. GitHub is used for version control and project documentation, while Visual Studio Code is used as the primary development environment. The website is intended to be hosted using Afrihost.

Stage 3: Responsive Design Implementation
The third stage focused on ensuring that the website works across different screen sizes.
•	Responsive media queries were implemented for smaller screens.
•	The main responsive breakpoints used in the CSS include:
o	Desktop: Above 900px
o	Tablet: 681px – 900px
o	Mobile: 680px and below
•	The layout changes from multi-column desktop layouts to more compact layouts on smaller screens.
Stage 4: Responsive Navigation
The navigation was adapted for smaller screen sizes.
On desktop screens, the navigation is displayed horizontally.
On smaller screens, the navigation changes into a mobile-friendly menu.
This prevents navigation links from becoming overcrowded and makes the website easier to use on mobile devices.
The mobile navigation was tested to ensure that users can still access all major website pages.
Stage 5: Visual Styling and Component Refinement
The final visual styling stage focused on improving consistency between website components.
The existing Hypnotic Beds appearance was maintained rather than introducing a completely different visual design.
Stage 6: Accessibility and Usability Improvements

These included:
Keyboard focus visibility using :focus-visible
Responsive media handling
Reduced-motion support
Improved text wrapping
Improved usability on smaller screens
Consistent button and link interaction states
These improvements were implemented without changing the normal visual identity of the website.

Phase 3: JavaScript Functionality, Interactivity and SEO Enhancements
Duration: 27 October 2026 – 31 October 2026
This phase focused on improving the functionality and usability of the Hypnotic Beds website by implementing JavaScript interactivity, form validation, gallery enhancements and search engine optimisation (SEO). The aim was to make the website more interactive, user-friendly and accessible while maintaining the existing Hypnotic Beds visual identity.
Stage 1: JavaScript Foundation and Integration
The first stage focused on implementing JavaScript functionality across the website.
The following improvements were made:
Created and maintained the external JavaScript file.
•	Linked the JavaScript file to the relevant HTML pages using the `defer` attribute.
•	Used the `DOMContentLoaded` event to ensure that JavaScript executes after the HTML document has been loaded.
•	Used JavaScript event listeners to respond to user interactions.
•	Organised JavaScript functionality into separate sections to improve code readability and  maintainability.
Stage 2: Responsive Navigation and Interactive Features
JavaScript was used to improve the website's navigation and interactive behaviour.
The following functionality was implemented:
•	Added a mobile navigation menu for smaller screen sizes.
•	Implemented a menu toggle to open and close the navigation.
•	Improved navigation usability on mobile and tablet devices.
•	Added interactive behaviour to website elements where required.
Stage 3: Quote and Order Form Validation
JavaScript validation was implemented on the Quote & Order page to help customers provide the required information correctly.
The following validation features were introduced:
•	Required field validation.
•	Customer name validation.
•	Phone number validation.
•	Gmail address format validation.
•	Quantity validation, allowing whole numbers between 1 and 100.
•	Delivery address validation on the order form.
•	Custom error messages displayed below incorrect fields.
•	Real-time validation when users type, change selections or leave fields.
•	Visual error indicators for invalid fields.
•	Automatic scrolling to the first invalid field when the form is submitted.
•	Success messages displayed after successful form validation.
The validation improves the accuracy of customer information and helps users identify and correct mistakes before completing a form.
Note: The current form functionality provides client-side validation and success messages. Actual transmission and storage of customer enquiries and orders require a connected form-handling service or backend.
Stage 4: Gallery Lightbox Functionality
An interactive image lightbox was implemented on the Gallery page to improve the presentation of product photographs.
The functionality includes:
•	Making gallery images clickable.
•	Enlarging selected images in a full-screen overlay.
•	Displaying image captions using the corresponding gallery item title.
•	Adding a close button to exit the enlarged image.
•	Allowing users to close the lightbox by clicking outside the image.
•	Supporting the Escape key to close the image viewer.
•	Supporting Enter and Space keyboard interactions to open images.
•	Preventing background page scrolling while the lightbox is open.
•	Providing responsive image sizing for smaller screens.
The lightbox allows visitors to view product photographs in greater detail without navigating away from the Gallery page.
Stage 5: Search Engine Optimisation (SEO)
SEO improvements were introduced to help search engines understand the content and purpose of the website pages.
The following improvements were implemented:
•	Added unique meta descriptions to the main website pages.
•	Created page-specific descriptions for the Home, Products & Prices, Services, Gallery, About, Contact and Quote & Order pages.
•	Included relevant business information and location details in appropriate descriptions.
•	Improved the relevance of page titles and descriptions to the content displayed on each page.
The meta descriptions provide search engines with summaries of individual pages and may be displayed in search results.
Stage 6: Accessibility and Usability Improvements
Additional improvements were made to support easier interaction with website features.
These included:
•	Keyboard accessibility for gallery images.
•	Visible focus indicators for interactive elements.
•	Accessible labels for the gallery lightbox controls.
•	Appropriate status messages for form validation results.
•	Reduced-motion support for animated interface elements.
•	Improved usability on smaller screens.
Stage 7: Testing and Final Refinements
The final stage focused on testing the new functionality and checking that it worked with the existing website design.
The following areas were checked:

•	Mobile navigation functionality.
•	Quote and order form validation.

Any identified issues were corrected during the refinement process.
